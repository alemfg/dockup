1. CRT PLUGIN — COMPLETE REWRITE
---------------------------------
Previous: 8AM 1H + 9:15 15M candles, Reversal only.
New: Bruce_Loki CRT methodology — prior session candles, two models.

- Range: PRIOR SESSION CANDLE (1AM or 5AM) not 8AM 1H candle
- TWO models: Reversal (5AM swept → reverse) + Continuation (1AM swept → follow)
- CRTModelClassifier: detects model by checking if 5AM swept 1AM range
- DOL (Draw on Liquidity): opposite side of swept range, model-dependent
- TP1 = 50% of range candle (tp1_pct_of_range, configurable)
- H4 manipulation check: flags high-volatility setups
- get_daily_state(): exposes 1AM/5AM ranges + model for dashboard

New config.yaml params (analysis.cr_9am):
  reversal_range: "5am", continuation_range: "1am"
  tp1_pct_of_range: 0.50, require_bos: true, require_fvg: false
  check_h4_manipulation: true
